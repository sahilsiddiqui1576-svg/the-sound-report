Playlists have quietly become the most powerful unit of distribution in the modern music industry — more influential, in aggregate, than any single radio station ever was.

## Key Points

- Editorial playlists build trust and control.
- Algorithmic playlists scale discovery.
- Independent playlists can change an artist's career.
