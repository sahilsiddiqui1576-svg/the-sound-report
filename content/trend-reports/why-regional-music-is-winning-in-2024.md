Regional-language music is no longer the niche category streaming platforms once treated it as — it's now consistently outperforming charts once dominated by Hindi and English releases.

## Key Takeaways

- Regional artists and playlists are the fastest-growing category on major platforms.
- Regional artists are going from niche to viral discovery engines.
- Platforms are now featuring more regional and ethnic languages than ever before.
