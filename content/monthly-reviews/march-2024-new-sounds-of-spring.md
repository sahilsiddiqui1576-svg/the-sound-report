March opened the year's music calendar with a genuine changing of the guard. Independent hip-hop crossed into the mainstream conversation, regional-language pop continued its climb up the charts, and a wave of singer-songwriters quietly delivered some of the most textured indie releases we've heard in years.

## Top Releases

This month's chart movement was led by breakout hip-hop, with regional pop and Punjabi crossover tracks not far behind — a trend we'll be tracking closely through the summer.

## Breakout Artists

A handful of independent acts posted their strongest streaming numbers to date, several of them entirely without major-label backing.
